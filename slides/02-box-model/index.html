How wide is the box? · 1

Teddy's box should be 200px wide

200px
280px
width: 200px;

width = how wide. Teddy is 200px wide. He fits!

padding: 30px;border: 10px solid brown;

Oh no! Now the box is 280px wide!

width was only for teddy. The stuffing and the wall are added outside it: 200 + 30 + 30 + 10 + 10 = 280px

This normal way is called content-box.

How wide is the box? · 2

The box is too wide! Make it 200px again

200px
280px
200px

We want the whole box to be 200px, wall to wall.

box-sizing: border-box;

It fits! The whole box is 200px.

The stuffing and the wall now stay inside the 200px, so teddy gets smaller: 200 − 30 − 30 − 10 − 10 = 120px

border-box: width = the whole box, up to the wall.
